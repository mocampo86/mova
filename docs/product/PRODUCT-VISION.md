# Product Vision

## One-liner

Reserva Canchas is a web platform that lets sports complex owners publish and manage their courts while players easily check availability and make reservations online.

## Problem

Sports complexes, clubs, and court owners still rely on phone calls, messages, or paper agendas to manage bookings. This is slow, error-prone, and creates friction for both owners and players.

## Target users

- **Players / end users**: people looking to book a court for a sport.
- **Complex owners / administrators**: people who manage one or more sports complexes.
- **Super administrators**: platform operators who support tenants and monitor the system.

## Vision statement

> Become the simplest, most reliable way to reserve sports courts in any discipline, giving complex owners full control over their availability while giving players a fast, mobile-first booking experience.

## Differentiators for the MVP

- Single platform for multiple sports, not just football.
- Mobile-first design that works on any device.
- Multi-complex support from day one with logical isolation.
- Recurring weekly reservations for fixed training sessions or leagues.
- Administrative tools to block users, block time slots, and manage reservations manually.

## Long-term direction

- Online payments and deposits (post-MVP).
- Notifications via email, SMS, or WhatsApp.
- Native mobile applications.
- Reputation, comments, and promotions.
- Marketplace with commission model (post-MVP).

## Success criteria

- A complex can be registered and start receiving reservations without phone calls.
- A player can register, find a court, and book a slot in under two minutes on mobile.
- Conflicting reservations are prevented automatically.
